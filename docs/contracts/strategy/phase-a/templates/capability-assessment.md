## Summary

Briefly state whether strategy engineering can continue and identify material degradations, review decisions, or blockers.

## Capability coverage

Describe each source-material requirement, its verified source, coverage, freshness, stability, replayability, and fallback. Distinguish actual probes from assumptions.

## Gaps and degradations

List unavailable, degraded, or untested capabilities. Explain the impact without inventing replacement data.

## Decisions requiring review

List material alternatives that require human choice. Do not silently convert them into strategy rules or thresholds.

## Probe evidence

Reference `capability-probes.json` and any immutable source artifacts. Do not include credentials or internal host paths.
